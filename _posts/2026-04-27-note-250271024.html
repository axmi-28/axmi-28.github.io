AI slop brings upon the students of today a peculiar realization of the utter laziness of humanity. It is worth to notice that this generation, the release of ChatGPT marking their highschool career, is likely the most fluent in AI language than any other complete population. 

They use AI as the perennial means of being lazy/cheating. Familiar with the shortfalls, the obvious and honestly repetitive syntax of AI writing, they sneakily used it wherever they can. But, as they should. Adolescents, with their yet to mature frontal cortex, have never been expected to be as responsible, principled as a mature adult. They have always been an “immature” population. 

Yet when they look look elsewhere, to the real world, to their teachers, to the “adults” they are supposed to become, they see the same. On the news, quotes that are immediately recognizable to their eyes as composed of AI. In school: on the slides, in presentations, in the assignments, so much is so obviously AI it makes you question how much the teacher is being paid for their work. The grade is AI written, the feedback is AI-written. The instagram feed, their twitter feed, either AI generated videos or obviously AI generated text. The menu of a restaurant uses AI text to describe their foods. At this point, this may feel like the senseless ranting of a particular sensitive teen. Perhaps, the issue is the fact that I am trying so hard to find these examples in everyday life. But to me, it has become physically impossible not to. 

The issue of AI prose is that, somehow, it has come to elicits an absolute hatred. The fact that it says so much without saying anything, the fact that it creates these comparisons that utterly lack reason, the fact that it goes so much over the top. That it just intuitively feels fake. 

The ultimate result of all of this is, unconsciously and consciously, is a gradual loss of respect for these supposed adults. We think: they are on the same level. These are full grown adults, who have decided themselves to lazy off, to shave an hour off of actual thinking in favor for a few minutes with their Gemini. They are cheating. They are bumbling idiots that believe we can’t see it for what it actually is.
